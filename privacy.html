Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

What data the app uses

  • TikTok: after you authorize the app, it receives an access token and your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) so it can show which account is connected, and uses the Content Posting API to upload the videos you approve.
  • YouTube: an access token and your channel name, used only to upload the videos you approve.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. No data is sent anywhere other than to TikTok and Google, through their official APIs.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect. Deleting the local token files, or revoking access in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or in your Google account, removes the app's access immediately.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 18.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
